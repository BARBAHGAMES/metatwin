a# MetaTwin

**The digital twin for builders — proof-of-capability infrastructure for the AI-native workforce.**

MetaTwin is MetaGlobe Technologies' B2B talent marketplace layer. It turns a builder's actual project work — starting with students and university builders — into a verified, portable Skills Passport that travels with them into the labour market.

## The thesis

The future of hiring isn't the résumé. It's the project. High school and university builders are already shipping real work using AI-assisted workflows — the problem is that none of it is captured, verified, or portable. MetaTwin closes that gap: it evidences what a builder actually did, how they did it, and what skills that proves.

The end state goes further than individual verification. MetaTwin is building toward a proof-of-capability marketplace where people aren't hired as individuals alone — they're hired *with* their AI workforce. The goal is to position Kenya as the first country where builders enter the job market already paired with their own AI agents: a workforce generation that shows up with both verified human capability and a working AI team.

Kenya is the proof point. Kenyans are already an outsized, vocal presence online — this is that same energy and visibility redirected into the next generation of how work gets proven and how talent gets hired.

## How it works

- **Capture** — project evidence from where builders actually work. GitHub first (proof-of-capability dashboard, piloting with Mount Kenya University), expanding to outreach records and pitch feedback.
- - **Verify** — behavioral and output data from real project work, esports/enterprise-skills competition activity, and club records feed into the MetaTwin Skills Registry.
  - - **Credential** — verified activity converts into a portable Skills Passport — not a single opaque score, but a tracked portfolio of evidence a builder owns and can carry anywhere.
    - - **Connect** — the B2B marketplace layer matches verified builders (and, going forward, their AI agents) to employers, positioning MetaTwin as the evidence layer between governments building national Skills Passports and labour-market institutions building skills taxonomies.
